Release 1.2.2 introduces the Cannon Block Palette customization menu, the "From Placement" aligner synchronization tool, and additional pearl cannon bugfixes:

### New Features

- **Block Palette & Colour Customization:**
  - Customize the building materials used in the generated return cannon schematic to match your base aesthetic.
  - Click the palette button next to "Generate Schem" in the Return Cannon menu to open the Block Palette editor.
  - Choose from 16 full-colour presets (White, Gray, Black, Red, Blue, Lime, etc.) or mix-and-match individual block types across 6 categories: Glass, Solid blocks, Stairs, Leaves, Terracotta, and Nether Wood (Crimson/Warped trapdoors and fence gates).
  - Built-in search and safety filtering ensure chosen blocks won't interfere with redstone or cannon mechanics.

- **Sync Settings From Aligner Placement ("From Placement"):**
  - Seamlessly configure the cannon by parsing an existing aligner schematic placement in Litematica.
  - Clicking the "From Placement" button automatically reads the placement's world coordinates, rotation, and mirror state, instantly syncing the cannon's start position, facing direction, and mirror mode, and recalculating firing solutions to match without needing to enter coordinates manually.

### Bugfixes & Improvements
- Additional pearl cannon bugfixes and stability improvements.
